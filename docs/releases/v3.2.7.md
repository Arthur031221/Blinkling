# Blinkling 3.2.7

A chat card whose model, effort and permission can be chosen, Auto explain as a quick bubble, a guide that stays on the page, a faster capture, and a card that always opens after an answer.

- Start Claude Code with `claude --dangerously-load-development-channels plugin:blinkling@blinkling`, then type `/blinkling:blinkling`. The flag lets Blink's chat reach this session. Claude Code asks you to confirm it once. Without it Blink still works and answers from its own session.
- The chat card can choose the model, the effort and the permission mode again. The lists were locked whenever the card could not read the session's current choice, which was most of the time. They now stay open and show what is chosen. A test opens a live session's card, while an answer is running and before a session has connected, and changes all three.
- Auto explain is a quick muttering bubble. It looks at the screen every second instead of every three, with a finer comparison and a shorter wait between answers, and asks the model only when something really changed. The answer is one or two short sentences in the language set in Blinkling's settings, shown in a small bubble next to Blink. The card does not open. The chat keeps a record of each one, with what was sent on the right and the answer on the left. It uses Sonnet 5.5 at low effort until you pick something else in the card, and the choice is kept. Chinese, Japanese and Korean text reaches the screen intact, which a test checks with writes split in the middle of a character.
- The Guide uses Sonnet 5.5 at low effort by default, changeable in the card. The empty block under the card when a guide starts is gone, and the card in guide mode is small and shows only Previous and Next.
- Guide marks are placed in page coordinates. With the browser helper, they are measured again when the page scrolls or the window is resized, so they stay on the text they were drawn on. Without the helper, marks stay where the screen had them, and the card says so.
- Each step's explanation sits under its mark. The marks have Previous and Next, and both scroll the page to the step. The marks stay when the card is moved or folded and go only on End guide. The line that offered to show the guide again is gone after End guide. The guide is recorded in the chat history in the usual layout.
- Blink thinks exactly while the model works, whether the work is a typed question, a capture, a chip, Auto explain or a guide, and stops on done or on a failure.
- When a capture starts, an open chat card closes first and Blink turns semi-transparent, so the screenshot has no card in it. The order stays the same: capture, the picture into the mouth, the card opens.
- Capture is faster from the press to the open card. Measured on this machine with the same test before and after, the whole run from the press to the card open went from about 3.2 to 3.5 seconds to about 2.0 seconds. The capture step itself did not get faster (about 0.4 seconds both times). The gain is in the eating and card steps: the mouth opens sooner, the picture flies in 400 ms instead of 600, and the eating motion is 1.8 times faster.
- After a typed question or a capture question is answered, the card now always opens by itself. It stayed shut when the card had been clicked away or had lost focus while the answer was on its way. Only Esc or the close button on that answer keeps it shut now. Both paths have a test.
- Nothing else changes from 3.2.6.
