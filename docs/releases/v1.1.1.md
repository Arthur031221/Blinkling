Glimpselet 1.1.1

The floating panel remembers its compact and expanded sizes and popup position across bridge restarts. Saved preferences contain window geometry, without questions, answers, images or session credentials.

Answers keep comparison tables readable in a scrollable area. External links open separately, and relative links cannot navigate away from the panel.

Interrupted answers keep their partial text with a cancellation or incomplete label. You can copy that text, and pinning is available after an answer completes. Clearing the panel or letting its session expire prevents late stream callbacks from restoring private text.

The screen preview distinguishes the last sent frame from the live view. Returning to the live preview preserves crop and redaction settings.

This release includes the rebuilt panel and bridge. Update the installed plugin using the repository installation instructions.
