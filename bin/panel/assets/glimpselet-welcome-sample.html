A tiny field guide to tea

Tea leaves change as they meet water. The same leaf can taste bright and grassy at a short steep, then round and honeyed a little later. Temperature, time, and the size of the leaf all matter.

Three useful starting points

  • Green tea: start with cooler water and a short steep to keep the taste fresh.
  • Oolong: try several small infusions; the aroma often changes from one cup to the next.
  • Black tea: hotter water and a longer steep make a fuller cup. Shorten the time if it tastes too strong.

お茶の味は、お湯の温度と蒸らす時間で変わります。短く蒸らすと、すっきりした香りを楽しめます。
