Your screen. A small question.

Blink waits in the corner
of your screen.

Hold Ctrl and click anything to ask about it. Answers come back from Blink, pointing at what they talk about.

Get started

Windows, macOS and Linux. Your Claude Code login. No publisher server.

Ctrl and click

Hold Ctrl and click a thing: a button, a paragraph, a window. The mini composer opens with three quick asks, and Enter sends the highlighted one.

Ctrl and drag

Keep Ctrl down and drag past a few pixels to take an area instead. The capture flies into the composer for your question.

Click Blink to talk

Click Blink to pick up the last conversation. Scroll back through earlier answers with the wheel.

Three actions

Start with Claude Code.

  1. Paste one line

    In a terminal, with Claude Code signed in:

    claude plugin marketplace add Arthur031221/Blinkling && claude plugin install blinkling@blinkling

  2. Run one command

    /blinkling:blinkling

    Blink appears on your desktop.

  3. Allow Screen Recording

    On a Mac, switch on Blinkling once in System Settings, Privacy & Security.

Store listings are not available yet. Blinkling installs from GitHub through Claude Code. Updates are manual. Open the install guide for your computer, with animated steps.

When you need more

Quiet extras.

The menu

Hover Blink for capture, a numbered guide through what is on screen, and auto explain, which is off until you turn it on.

Hotkey and full composer

A global hotkey opens the capture overlay. Long messages grow into the full composer, where you can choose the model and effort.

With the browser companion

Translate a web page in place, open PDFs in a reader, and capture a whole page. Without the companion, these simply stay out of the way.

Inside Claude Code

Blink starts with your Claude Code session. Slash commands and screen tools let Claude look at the screen when you ask.

Nothing leaves your computer until you ask a question. Pixels and text you pick are sent through your Claude Code login, and your selected provider's terms apply. Password fields and blocked sites pause auto explain. Read what is sent and stored.
